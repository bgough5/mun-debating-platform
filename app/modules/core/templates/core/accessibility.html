Accessibility

This platform aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA (decision D-018).

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone. Use Tab to move, Enter or Space to act. A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on every page.
  • Positions and votes are always shown as words and symbols as well as colour (for example a tick icon with the word "Yes").
  • Timers can be paused and extended by the chair, including extra time for an individual student (WCAG 2.2.1).
  • Live changes (speakers list, timers, votes) are announced to screen readers without flooding them.
  • Pages work at 200% zoom and on narrow screens. "Easy-read text" at the bottom of every page widens letter and line spacing.
  • Animations are switched off if your device asks for reduced motion.

If something doesn't work for you, tell your teacher: it is a bug and we will fix it.
