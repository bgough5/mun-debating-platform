Privacy

This platform runs on a teacher's laptop at South Oakleigh College. It stores only:

  • your first name (and the first letter of your surname only if two people share a first name),
  • your class, delegation and role,
  • your debating and Model UN records (speeches, scores, milestones),
  • which device you joined from (a random code, not your IP address or anything about the device).

No surnames, emails, birthdays or photos. Nothing is sent to any outside service. AI features are off unless the school approves them, and even then no names or student writing are sent without that approval.

Class data is archived at the end of each year. Your pathway record is kept while you are in the program and deleted on request or when you leave.
