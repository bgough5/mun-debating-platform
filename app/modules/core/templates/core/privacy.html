Privacy

This platform runs on a teacher's laptop at South Oakleigh College. It stores only:

  • your first name (and the first letter of your surname only if two people share a first name),
  • your class, delegation and role,
  • your debating and Model UN records (speeches, scores, milestones),
  • which device you joined from (a random code, not your IP address or anything about the device).

No surnames, emails, birthdays or photos. Nothing is sent to any outside service. AI features work offline unless the school approves an outside AI service; your name is never sent, and your writing is sent only if the school has approved that.

Class data is archived at the end of each year. Your pathway record is kept while you are in the program. When you leave, or if you or your family ask, the coordinator erases your name and your own writing. Anything deleted is removed for good after 30 days, and the record of who changed what is kept for one year.
