{{ icon("student") }} Student quick-start

  1. Sign in. On your laptop, open the address on your sign-in card, or go to the address on the board and type the code under your name. Your code already knows who you are.
  2. Your page. "My page" shows your delegation, tasks, live sessions, debates and practice.
  3. In a live session. The stage tracker shows where the committee is. "What you can do now" tells you what to do: answer the roll, add yourself to the speakers list, raise or second a motion, predict votes in a whip count, and vote.
  4. Debating. Your draws and results appear on your page. You see only your own scores and feedback.
  5. Prep and practise. Build a debate case and rebut the other side's likely arguments, write speeches from templates, and practise impromptu, prepared or rebuttal speeches against the clock; also a practice committee against computed delegations; a practice negotiation with a rule-based partner (not AI, unless your school has approved an AI service).
  6. If something goes wrong. Lost your card or code? Your teacher can show it to you or give you a new one. Signing in on another laptop signs you out of the first.

The platform never writes your speech, clause or amendment. Predictions are estimates, not facts. Glossary · Privacy
