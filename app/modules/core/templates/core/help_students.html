{{ icon("student") }} Student quick-start

  1. Join. Scan the QR code on your join card, or go to the address on the board and type your class code. Tap your own first name. Your device stays signed in as you.
  2. Your page. "My page" shows your delegation, tasks, live sessions, debates and practice.
  3. In a live session. The stage tracker shows where the committee is. "What you can do now" tells you what to do: answer the roll, add yourself to the speakers list, raise or second a motion, predict votes in a whip count, and vote.
  4. Debating. Your draws and results appear on your page. You see only your own scores and feedback.
  5. Practise. Timed speech practice with a rubric self-review; a practice committee against computed delegations; a practice negotiation with an AI delegation (offline unless your school has approved an AI service).
  6. If something goes wrong. Wrong name? Tell your teacher, who can release it. "Waiting for a teacher"? Your name is in use on another device: your teacher approves it.

The platform never writes your speech, clause or amendment. Predictions are estimates, not facts. Glossary · Privacy
