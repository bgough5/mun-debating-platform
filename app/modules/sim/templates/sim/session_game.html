        Your instructions

        Secret, from your capital. Rivals have their own.

          Regional groups

          Win most of a group and it endorses your draft: a bonus each turn, and its members lean your way at the vote.

            The draft

            The working group only changes someone else's clause if the room supports the change, and only strikes a clause it wouldn't pass now.

              The drafting table

              Propose a clause from a card. You may put it in your own words; it's judged only by what it commits countries to.

                Click a country on the map, or choose one here.

                Side event

                Host a regional group: every delegation in it warms to you a little. 2 actions.

                Bloc whip

                The powers in the room

                You and each rival: Influence to spend, how much of the draft is theirs, the regional groups and sponsors behind them, and how busy they were last turn.

                  Votes so far

                    The sign-up sheets

                    The clerk's sheets: a draft is tabled when it has its sponsors and enough signatories. The first tabled, as L.1, is voted on first.

                      Bloc meetings

                      The meeting rooms

                      Every regional group, its members and where they lean. More than half of a group's voters endorse a draft.

                        Your whip sheet

                        Mark how you think each delegation will vote on each clause (C1, C2…) and on the whole text: click to cycle Y (yes), A (abstain), N (no). It's free. At the summit every mark is checked against the real vote: the Kingmaker's path.

                        Your staff

                        Post each adviser to study a delegation or a rival. They report at the start of each turn; juniors can be wrong, and say how sure they are. Reports that prove right earn promotions.
