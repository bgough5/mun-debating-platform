How this is calculated

Each country's sources give a position and a weight (official statements and recorded votes count most, the archetype model estimate least). The weighted average is the country's expected position; the spread shrinks as evidence grows. Members of a bloc share part of their randomness (cohesion), so the simulation doesn't treat them as independent coin flips. The simulation runs the vote {{ r.runs }} times under the committee's rule. Model version {{ pred.model_version_id }}; inputs hash {{ pred.inputs_hash[:12] }}. These are estimates, not facts: check the sources.
